## 如何理解这些笔记 / How to Interpret the Notes

我尽量分别标明监管批准、正式论文、企业披露、试验登记和个人分析，并保留人群、剂量、随访时间及统计口径。不同研究中的百分比不一定可以直接比较；增加瘦体重也不自动意味着改善力量或日常功能。

I try to distinguish regulatory approvals, published studies, company disclosures, trial registrations, and my own interpretations, while retaining population, dose, follow-up, and statistical context. Percentages from different studies are not necessarily directly comparable, and increased lean mass does not automatically establish better strength or daily function.

资料分批整理与核查，具体日期和版本以对应页面及来源为准。批准状态、安全性信息、价格和开发计划可能变化；这份笔记不是实时数据库。尚未核实或尚未披露的内容，不应理解为不存在或结果为零。

Sources were collected and checked at different times; refer to the dates and versions shown with each item. Approval status, safety information, prices, and development plans may change. These notes are not a live database. Information that is unverified or undisclosed should not be interpreted as absent or zero.

其中的 CAPA 方案、练习题和拟定行动属于学习用途，不代表相关企业实际采用的内部记录或措施。

CAPA plans, exercises, and proposed actions are educational examples, rather than representations of the companies' actual internal records or procedures.
